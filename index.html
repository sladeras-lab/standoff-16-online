How it works

Free-for-all deathmatch to 15 kills, up to 8 players, respawn after 3 seconds, ammo is unlimited. Every player is a real person, there are no bots.

Host: press Host a game, then send the invite link or the 5-letter code. Keep your tab open, because you are the server and the room closes when you leave.

Join: type the code, or open the invite link, then press Join.

Connections are peer to peer, so there is no game server to pay for. Strict corporate or school networks can block them.
